API reference

Doqua.Controls namespace

Ready-made controls in the classic style: panels, labels, buttons, check and radio buttons, text inputs, lists, tables, trees, tabs, menus, date and number inputs.

Classes

ButtonPush button with centered text, drawn in the classic 3D style: a raised edge, a sunken frame while pressed (the text moves 1 px down and right), a black frame and dotted rectangle when focused, and embossed grey text when disabled. Raises Button.Click on a left-button click or Enter/Space.
CheckBoxCheck box with a title, drawn in the classic style: a sunken 13 x 13 box with a check mark. A left click (on the box or the text) or Space while focused toggles CheckBox.Checked.
ComboBox<T>Drop-down list in the classic style: a sunken field showing the selected item and an arrow button; clicking opens the list below (or above) it. Items are values of type T, shown with ComboBox<T>. The text cannot be edited.
ComboBoxItemCollection<T>Items of a ComboBox<T>; changes keep the combo box's selection consistent.
DateInputDate field in the classic style: a read-only field showing DateInput.Value with DateInput.Format, and a button that opens a calendar. The calendar picks a day; its title switches to month and year grids. Dates outside [DateInput.Min, DateInput.Max] cannot be chosen.
FileFilterA named set of file name patterns for FileInput, e.g. new FileFilter("Certificates", "*.pem", "*.crt"). Patterns use * (any characters) and ? (one character) and are matched against the file name only, ignoring case on every platform.
FileInputA path field with a "..." button that opens a file browser below the field (above it if there is no room). The path can also be typed. FileInput.Error tells whether it satisfies the options (FileInput.MustExist, FileInput.Required, FileInput.MatchFilter); invalid text is drawn in FileInput.InvalidColor.
GroupBoxA Panel with a classic etched frame and a GroupBox.Title on its top edge, grouping related controls. Children are placed as in a panel, relative to the group's top-left corner: leave GroupBox.ContentTop pixels at the top for the title. Disabling the group disables everything in it.
InputSingle-line text editor.
LabelSingle- or multi-line ('\n') text drawn with one font and color.
MenuClosedEventArgsResult of a closed PopupMenu.
MenuItemAn item of a PopupMenu (or a separator).
NumberInputInteger input with classic up / down buttons (a "spin box"). NumberInput.Value always lies in [NumberInput.Min, NumberInput.Max]. Typing accepts only digits (and a leading '-' when Min is negative) and refuses numbers that can no longer fit the range; a number below Min is allowed while typing and is corrected when the field loses focus or on Enter. The buttons (repeating while held), Up / Down (by NumberInput.Step), PageUp / PageDown (10 steps) and the mouse wheel change the value.
PanelContainer for other controls, positioned by their own X/Y (or Control.Anchor). With Panel.ScrollBars set, the content (the children's right and bottom edges) can be larger than the panel: classic scroll bars appear when needed, the mouse wheel scrolls (Shift+wheel horizontally), and a child that gets the keyboard focus is scrolled into view. Anchors then refer to the visible area, without the scroll bars, so stretched children never make the panel scroll.
PopupMenuMenu shown on top of a window's content, in the classic style. It closes when an item is chosen, on a click outside it, on Escape, or when the window is deactivated or resized; then PopupMenu.Closed reports the chosen item, or null. Items: mouse (left or right button), Up/Down and Enter. Open it with PopupMenu.Show or through Control.ContextMenu.
RadioButtonOption button of a RadioGroup, drawn in the classic style: a sunken circle with a dot. RadioButton.Checked is read from the group. Clicking or Space selects the button; the arrow keys select the next or previous button of the group; the whole group is a single Tab stop. Create RadioButton<T> instances.
RadioButton<T>Radio button carrying a RadioButton<T> for its RadioGroup<T>.
RadioGroupNon-visual owner of a set of RadioButtons: at most one of them is selected, and the group, not the buttons, holds that state. The buttons can be anywhere in the control tree. Use RadioGroup<T> to give each button a value.
RadioGroup<T>Radio group whose buttons carry values of type T:
RectangleRectangle filled with a solid color.
SplitContainerTwo panels separated by a bar that the user drags to share the space between them. Panel1 keeps its size (SplitContainer.SplitterDistance) when the container is resized; Panel2 gets the rest. Neither panel becomes smaller than its minimum size.
TabControlPages with a strip of tabs on top, drawn in the classic (Windows 9x/2000) style. The selected page fills the area below the tabs; the others are hidden. Disabled pages cannot be selected and their tabs are grey; if every page is disabled, no page is selected or shown. Keys (while focus is inside): Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+PageDown next page, Ctrl+Shift+Tab / Ctrl+PageUp previous.
TableGrid of text cells with a header row, in the classic style. Table.Columns define the headers and widths; Table.Rows hold the cells (see also Table.SetData and Table.AddRow). Every cell uses the table's Table.Font and Table.Color unless its own are set. Drag a header border to resize a column, double-click it to fit the column to its contents. Scroll bars appear when the cells do not fit; the mouse wheel scrolls (Shift+wheel horizontally).
TableCellA cell of a Table. TableCell.Font and TableCell.Color are null (use the table's) unless the program redefines them for this cell.
TableCellEventArgsA click on a Table: the cell's indexes, or null outside the cells.
TableColumnA column of a TableColumn.Table: its header text and width.
TableColumnCollectionColumns of a Table.
TableContextMenuEventArgsThe context menu of a Table is about to open for a cell (or outside the cells: null indexes).
TableRowA row of a TableRow.Table: its cells, in column order.
TableRowCollectionRows of a Table.
TableRowEventArgsA row of a Table was activated (double click, or Enter on the selected row).
TabPageA page of a TabControl: a Panel whose TabPage.Title is shown on its tab. While it belongs to a TabControl, its Control.Visible and Control.Bounds are managed by the TabControl; set Control.Enabled to false to make it unselectable.
TabPageCollectionPages of a TabControl, in tab order.
TextAreaMulti-line text editor in the classic style (sunken edge, vertical scroll bar when needed).
ToggleControlBase of CheckBox and RadioButton: a small classic box followed by text. A left click (on the box or the text) or Space while focused calls ToggleControl.OnActivated.
TreeNodeA node of a TreeNode.TreeView: its text, its child TreeNode.Nodes and any application data in TreeNode.Tag.
TreeNodeCollectionChildren of a TreeNode, or the top-level nodes of a TreeNodeCollection.TreeView.
TreeViewHierarchy of TreeNodes in the classic style: dotted lines, [+] / [-] boxes and a highlighted selection. Click a node to select it, its box (or double-click it) to expand or collapse it. Keys: Up / Down / Home / End / PageUp / PageDown move the selection; Right expands (then goes to the first child); Left collapses (then goes to the parent); Enter toggles. Scroll bars appear when needed.

Static classes

MessageBoxA modal message window: an icon, the text (wrapped to fit) and buttons, in a small dialog window centred over its owner (see Window.ShowModal). While it is open the owner gets no input; Show returns at once and the result arrives in its callback.
